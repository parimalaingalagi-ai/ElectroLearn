Practise technical fundamentals and professional, scenario-based questions. Explain your assumptions, diagnostics, trade-offs and validation plan as you would in an engineering interview.

Open Professional Question Bank →

What strong technical answers include

  • Clarify the requirement and state assumptions before jumping to a solution.
  • Explain the principle, key equation or block diagram behind your decision.
  • Compare realistic trade-offs such as accuracy, cost, power, timing and complexity.
  • Describe how you would test normal, boundary and failure conditions.

Practice prompts are original engineering scenarios, not official or leaked employer questions.
